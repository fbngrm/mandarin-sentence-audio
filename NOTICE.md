# Sources and reuse

## AISHELL-3 recordings and transcripts — Apache License 2.0

Beijing Shell Shell Technology Co., Ltd. Original corpus:
https://www.openslr.org/93/

See `licenses/AISHELL3-Apache-2.0.txt` and the original documentation in
`licenses/AISHELL3-original-readme.txt`.

Modifications: original WAV recordings converted to mono 24 kHz, 48 kbps MP3.
The speech is unchanged. Original recording IDs, speaker IDs, Chinese transcripts,
and numeric-tone pinyin annotations are retained. Pinyin may contain original
annotation errors; it is not generated by speech recognition.

Reference: Yao Shi, Hui Bu, Xin Xu, Shaoji Zhang, Ming Li,
*AISHELL-3: A Multi-speaker Mandarin TTS Corpus and the Baselines*.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.11567

## Tatoeba sentence text — CC BY 2.0 France

Sentence text is by the Tatoeba contributors named in each record's `by` field.
The `url` field links to the original sentence and its history. A null author
means the export has no current owner; consult the linked history for credits.
Retain these credits and links when redistributing or displaying sentences.

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/
Terms: https://tatoeba.org/en/terms_of_use
Source: https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/cmn/cmn_sentences_detailed.tsv.bz2
Export last modified: 2026-10-10. Downloaded: 2026-10-15.

No Tatoeba recordings are distributed. Their audio licenses are separate.
Text is an unfiltered corpus export, not a reviewed set of learning examples.
